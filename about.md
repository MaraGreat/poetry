### [Mara Great](https://maragreat.github.io/poetry) | Poet 
Mara Great has been writing since she was a child but never thought of herself as a poet.  About three years ago, she started taking her poetry-self seriously, starting with a poem as a present for her younger sister’s birthday. 

Mara loves writing about nature as well as learning about the craft of poetry from fellow writers.  Mara resides in the DC area, with her husband and their dog.
