layout: poem
title: Crashing Life
author: Mara Great 
---

- My friend -64- full of life, 
- just finished a 45-mile bike ride. 

- Crashed into a parked car. 

- His broken body - on life support. 
- His heart beating 
- still, his blood flowing
- still, his lungs breathing
- mechanically. 
- The support was cut off. 
- His organs donated. 

- Dale's death froze my heart
- for a moment, then
- it brutally 
- continued to beat. 

- Almost obsessively, I keep
- re-reading his obituary. 
- Foolishly believing
- I can hold on to him that way. 

- So much
- I did not get to know. 
- Your middle name
- was Timothy.
