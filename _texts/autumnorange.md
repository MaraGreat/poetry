---
layout: poem
title: Autumn Orange / Old Gold 
author: Mara Great
---

- The autumn forest glows orange, 
- rusty leaves lavishly strewn about. 
- Trees shoot up thin and bare:
- elm and oak holding on to their fading foliage
- faintly flaming into the frost. 
---
- The copper creek cheerfully clatters, 
- weaves waves around erratic rocks. 
- Silky sunlight strokes only treetops, 
- shining like old gold. 
---
- I veer past on my bike
- inhale the sunlit landscape, 
- freeze out any thought of impending darkness and cold. 
---
- This is no preparation for
- how deeply I'll miss this gold
- during winter, when I'll need
- it's warm glow most. 
---
