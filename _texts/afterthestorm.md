---
layout: poem
title: After the Storm
author: Mara Great
---

- Under a hard blue sky,
- over the street's shint leaf covered surface
- like a page out of Emily Dickenson's 
- herbarium,
- I bike. 


- Rays pierce through veiled foliage. 
- To my left, the sun rapidly rolls
- over the tall treetops.
- Wild water gushes, 
- covering up human garbage
- stuck between rocks' crevices
- and beaver dam's branched. 


- High water unable to offer me
- even temporary relief
- from the raging reality 
- of cruelty and crushing
- destruction.
