layout: poem
title: Freeing Flies
author: Mara Great
---

- Before I slow down and write, 
- let me take the garbage outside. 
- Before I walk to my desk and sit, 
- let me rinse the dishes, quick!
- The hand soap needs a refill - 
- did anybody text or email?
---
- My body moves past the bathroom door mirror
- I catch my gaze and wonder:
- "Do I keep moving so I can hide
- and trap my thought and rhymes inside?"
- My body and I keep walking around somehow, 
- I am afraid of the silence that comes when I sit down. 
---
- Mind over body (sigh) I'm sitting. 
- I hear ticking-- four flied bussing their bodies flicking
- against the wide surface of the shiny window.
- I need to get up and free them, right now!
- I slide open the screen to give them an out- 
- they don't want to be freed and continue to be loud. 
---
- At least I can set my thoughts free: I know I'll feel lighter
- Seeing my pen moving and flowing over paper. 
- Letters and words, gliding and flying
- Out of my head, my heart my hand. Describing
- this mini moment in time. 
- It will now be - forever mine...
