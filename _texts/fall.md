*Reworked September 2024* 

- I stumbled
- bare arms flailing
- through the crisp autumn air. 
- The concrete pavement
- moving quickly
- toward me. 
- I crash. 
- My hands, 
- now scratched and bloody, 
- cushioned my body 
- in the fall. 
- Luckily, 
- I did not break
- A limb. 

- Had I been 
- The tree, I'd stand erect, 
- firmly rooted in the soft soil
- of the earth. 
- Swaying my bendy branches
- in the whistling wind. 
- Or, had I been a lead, 
- I'd flutter
- slowly and graciously
- onto the sidewalk,
- joining the other leaves. 
- And, I'd change colors
- painlessly.
